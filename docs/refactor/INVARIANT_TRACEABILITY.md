Status meanings: `EVIDENCED` means current repository evidence directly covers the invariant;
`PARTIAL` means useful behavior exists but canonical end-to-end proof is incomplete; `CONFLICT`
means current authority must be replaced on the critical path. A status is not a completion claim.

## CP-01 reading rule

The repeated owner and gap entries are intentional: invariants are individually traceable while
their implementation is delivered in cohesive vertical slices. Each later slice must replace its
rows with exact symbol/test/live-acceptance evidence before claiming `VERIFIED` or `MIGRATED`.
